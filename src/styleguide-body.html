07 — Voice

Facts, not adjectives

Short declaratives. Lead with the concrete thing, then one sentence of substance. Numbers and credentials carry the copy: 5th degree, since 2003, ages 5 and up.

Do

"Book ahead, borrow a gi, take a class. Talk pricing after."

Concrete, sequential, no promises the mat has not earned.

Don't

"Unlock your potential and transform your life on the mats!"

Adjectives doing the work of facts. No exclamation marks anywhere.

01

Sentence case for content

Uppercase is structural only: nav, buttons, eyebrows, labels — always with wide tracking.

02

Jiu-jitsu vocabulary, untranslated

Gi, no-gi, rolling, open mat, positional sparring, professor. Never glossed.

03

Baldwin is always named

The town appears in every page title, hero and meta description. It is the search term.

07 — Rules

How to use it

Color

Accent red is for action and emphasis only — never a background for body copy. Cream is the alternating section band. Two background colors per page, maximum.

Type

Headings are always uppercase Titillium Web. Never set body copy in Titillium, and never set a heading in Plex.

Shape

Zero border radius everywhere. Hairline borders instead of shadows. The grid does the work, not the decoration.
